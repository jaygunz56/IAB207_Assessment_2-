Acknowledgement of Country

Traditional Custodians

Every event on Triumph Motorevents takes place on Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander Country. This page explains how to acknowledge that on your event listing.

Acknowledgement vs Welcome to Country

An Acknowledgement of Country is a short statement of respect for the Traditional Custodians of the land. Anyone can give one, which is why organisers can add one to their event listing here.

A Welcome to Country is different. It can only be given by Traditional Owners of that land, so if you'd like one at your event you'll need to contact the local Traditional Owner group directly.

Finding the Traditional Custodians for your venue

  • Start with the AIATSIS Map of Indigenous Australia to get a general idea of the language and nation groups in the area.
  • The map only shows general locations, so check with the local Land Council, Traditional Owner group or your local council before naming anyone.
  • Many racetracks and venues already use an Acknowledgement of Country, so it's worth asking them what they use.
  • If you can't confirm who the Traditional Custodians are, or there is more than one group with a connection to the land, use the generic acknowledgement instead of naming a group.

Options on Triumph Motorevents

Generic

Triumph Motorevents acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the land on which this event is held and pays respect to Elders past and present.

Enhanced

Triumph Motorevents acknowledges the [Traditional Custodians] people, the Traditional Custodians of the land on which this event is held, and pays respect to their Elders past and present.
