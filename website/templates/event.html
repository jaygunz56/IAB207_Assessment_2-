Queensland Drift Championship

A full day of drifting competition at Queensland Raceway.

Date

17 October 2026

Time

10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Venue

Queensland Raceway

Location

Willowbank, Queensland

About the event

The Queensland Drift Championship brings drivers from across the state together for a full day of competitive drifting at Queensland Raceway.

Spectators can watch qualifying sessions, tandem battles and the final competition rounds throughout the day. Food vendors and spectator areas will be available around the circuit.

Event information

  • Gates open at 9:00 AM
  • All ages welcome
  • Free spectator parking available
  • Food and drinks available at the venue
  • Participant and spectator tickets available
